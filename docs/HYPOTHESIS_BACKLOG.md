**Tiers (research first):**
- **S (v ≥ 8):** strong story, data ready, high capacity, diversifying. Start here.
- **A (v 6.5-7.9):** solid, mostly data-ready.
- **B (v 5-6.4):** promising but data-hungry, crowded, or capacity-limited.
- **C (v < 5):** exploratory / speculative / needs data we may not have.
